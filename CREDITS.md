## Sound

The sea, the wind and the footsteps are recordings from the Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundles (sonniss.com), under their
licence: free to use in a game, not to pass on as files. They are not part of this block's open licence, and are not in
its repo: `MISSING_ASSETS.md` there names each one. The libraries: Discover Oregon (Ocean and Stream; Wind and Storms)
and Levan Nadashvili (Civilian Footsteps; Soldier Footsteps).

The full list with sources is in `LICENCE-NOTES.md`.
